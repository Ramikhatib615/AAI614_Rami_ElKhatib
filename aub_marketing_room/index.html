Executive brief — Chair

The brief appears here once the managers have reported.

Figures briefed into the managers are drawn from AUB's registrar and admissions pages, reporting on Lebanon's higher-education funding crisis (L'Orient Today, LCPS, Xinhua), and 2026 higher-education marketing benchmarks (Search Influence, RC Strategies, KEG/UCAS). The managers cite them; they are told to label anything else as an estimate.

This is a decision-support tool, not an oracle. Six confident managers can be wrong together. Treat the brief as a structured starting argument and check any number before it reaches a real budget.
